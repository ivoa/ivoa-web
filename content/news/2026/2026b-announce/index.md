The IVOA _Southern Spring_ November 2026 Interoperability Meeting will 
take place in Perth (Australia) and will be hosted at The Hub, in the 
Bentley Technology Park in South Perth. The meeting will be organised 
and run by the ASVO and Data Central and will run from Friday 6th to
Sunday 8th November 2026.

You can already (freely) 
[register](https://events.humanitix.com/perth-ivoa-interoperability-meeting)
for the event, follow logistics on the organser's 
[web page](https://adass2026.icrar.org/ivoa-2026), and stay tuned for the
detailed 
[program on the IVOA wiki](https://wiki.ivoa.net/twiki/bin/view/IVOA/InterOpNov2026).

Program times are reported on Perth's locale.

Looking forward to seeing you all in Perth!
